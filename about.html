About Achievement Planner

A practical companion for actually finishing your Steam achievement lists, not just tracking how far you got.

What This Is

Steam tells you which achievements exist and which ones you have. It doesn't tell you the fastest order to get them, which ones you'll miss forever if you're not careful, or how long a game will actually take to 100%. AchievementPlanner exists to fill in that gap: a curated, Steam-verified achievement plan for every game in its catalog, with recommendations, session-length estimates, and written guides for the achievements that need more than a description to make sense.

How It Works

Browse the catalog and open any game to see its full achievement list, difficulty rating, and estimated completion time. Log in with Steam to see which achievements you've already unlocked and get a real recommendation for what to go for next. Track your progress as XP, levels, and badges that follow your account, not just this browser. Climb the Podiums leaderboards against every other AchievementPlanner player.

Your Steam Data

AchievementPlanner never sees your Steam password - logging in goes through Steam's own login page. Most of what you see is read live from Steam each time you need it; the only thing actually saved is a small progress/stats snapshot tied to your Steam ID. See the Steam Login & Your Data guide for the exact details.
